About

I am a researcher in trustworthy AI and machine learning security, currently pursuing a PhD at Ulm University in collaboration with Huawei Research (Paris and Munich). My work sits at the intersection of formal reasoning, probabilistic uncertainty, and real-world ML deployments.

My core research integrates Subjective Logic and Bayesian deep learning into neural networks, enabling models to reason explicitly about their own uncertainty, propagate trust through multi-agent systems, and remain interpretable under adversarial conditions.

Beyond academia, I have worked at Huawei, Nokia Bell Labs, Mithril Security and INRIA, building production-grade ML systems and defenses against real-world attacks. I contribute to the EU HORIZON projects CONNECT, CASTOR and DUCA.
